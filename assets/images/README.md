# Khidmat illustrative service images

Created on 9 October 2026 using the built-in `image_gen` tool, not the CLI. Originals remain in the generation directory; selected images were copied into this project. These are illustrative marketing/category assets, not photographs of registered workers, verification evidence or portfolio work. Actual public worker photos must remain separate.

## Hero — `khidmat-team-hero.png`

Final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide mobile service marketplace hero cover background for Khidmat, a Pakistani local-worker app. Create a premium editorial photograph, landscape 3:2 composition. Three adult Pakistani skilled tradespeople in a tidy sunlit courtyard outside a contemporary Pakistani home: an electrician in teal work shirt holding an insulated toolbox, a plumber in navy workwear with pipe wrench at his side, and a woman professional home cleaner in modest teal uniform holding a small cleaning caddy. Natural, respectful, approachable expressions and realistic local appearance, safe equipment, authentic work clothes. Frame waist-up, team grouped toward right side, generous quiet cream wall negative space on left so app can overlay headings, softly blurred plants and masonry, warm daylight, teal and cream palette. Crisp photographic textures with calm restrained commercial art direction, not illustration. No writing, no text, no logo, no watermarks, no verification badges. Marketing illustration only, not fake worker profile photography.
